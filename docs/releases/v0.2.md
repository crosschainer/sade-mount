# v0.2

Finding something to spawn is easier, NPC hits feel like hitting a person, and car drivers now hold the wheel.

- **Organized assets:** NPCs, Vehicles, Common buildings, Ramps, Street props and Construction props have their own folders. CJ and Big Smoke appear first. **All** keeps the complete source model collection available, including models outside the curated selection. Assets still load on demand, and existing saved references keep working.
- **NPC hit feedback:** crowbars and other Sandbox weapons now get flesh impact sounds, blood effects and wound decals from NPCs. Physgun posing still works.
- **Car poses:** drivers grip steering wheels, and occupants' legs and feet extend forward into the footwell. Targets come from the car's wheel and floor geometry. This supports the Human avatar; seats without usable targets keep their normal pose.

To update, close s&box and replace `sbox/mount/sade` with the `sade` folder from the ZIP. Keep your `sade.json` if you have customized it, then launch s&box and activate the mount in **Mounts**.

GTA San Andreas — The Definitive Edition must remain installed separately. The download contains the mount and its dependencies, with no game data or s&box engine files.
