The scripts below are trusted `run_csharp` probes for an editor with the matching source build of the mount installed. CuratedBrowser.cs, CuratedScenery.cs and PedestrianHitFeedback.cs cover the next unreleased update; they require changes that are not in the public v0.1 ZIP.

CuratedBrowser.cs audits the mounted registry without decoding models. It checks the 322 curated choices (67 NPCs, 191 vehicles and 64 scenery models) plus the complete static/skeletal source model collection under All, the seven intended folders, CJ/Big Smoke priority, unique paths and agreement between the folder tree and Sandbox's model list. The recursive root list includes both collections. Source weapon models are available through All; legacy aliases and generated loadout/animation variants stay hidden while saved references still resolve. Run it before CuratedScenery.cs when measuring how many models were already decoded during browsing.

CuratedScenery.cs loads all 64 curated buildings, ramps and props in a disposable scene. It checks finite bounds, renderable geometry and nonempty collision, then captures a private overview with each model scaled to fit its own cell. The overview is for reviewing the selection, not for comparing real-world sizes. The active scene and camera are left unchanged.

PedestrianHitFeedback.cs runs in the official Sandbox project so it can invoke the actual MeleeWeapon impact override. With mounted CJ and Big Smoke in a temporary scene, it checks flesh surfaces on the 16-body rigs and fallback capsules, the stock melee flesh sound, one blood burst and one wound decal, damage and retaliation, and no duplicated effects from bullet damage. It repairs simulated legacy generic collider surfaces without rebuilding or moving the rig and verifies that frozen limbs remain frozen. It calls the real impact methods and damage hook without injecting a key press; multiplayer behavior is not covered.

VehiclePhysics.cs is a trusted `run_csharp` script for a running s&box editor with the current SADE mount installed. Discover `run_csharp` with the CLI, then pass the script's absolute path. It loads six real mounted vehicle families into an isolated temporary scene, advances the engine's physics, checks source mass, ground balance, propulsion, flotation and takeoff, and destroys the temporary scene. It leaves the active scene and camera unchanged. The disabled test driver supplies deterministic inputs through the mount controller; seat interaction is a separate live test.

BikePlaneDynamics.cs checks parked upright balance and mirrored left/right turns on the real Sanchez and BMX: inward lean, lateral velocity, upright stability and bounded suspension motion. It also checks that a stationary Dodo falls, a banked wing turns toward its lowered side, and a runway takeoff requires forward airspeed. This uses deterministic controls in a private physics scene and does not establish keyboard feel or multiplayer behavior. Its synthetic driver is placed far away because PlayerController creates active physics components even when the controller itself is disabled.

RoadBrakingSuspension.cs checks Infernus against its installed suspension equilibrium, rendered tire contact, bounded bump/droop travel and chassis clearance. At 110 km/h it distinguishes coasting, all-wheel foot braking and rear-only handbraking, with measured stopping time/distance and chassis stability. This private-scene test supplies controller state directly; it does not establish that a user's physical S/Space input was received. The installed-source Vehicle tests separately check inherited invisible vehicle-material alpha and source brake bias.

VehicleSeats.cs checks the door selection used by E: left driver/right passenger, rotated vehicles, occupied seats, entry distance, and bike rider/pillion access from both sides. It also checks engine sitting mode, disabled seated collision, passenger/driver separation, clear exits, and restored walking physics. It uses the actual mounted Infernus and NRG500 prefabs with engine PlayerControllers in a temporary scene. Offline it exercises the mount's entry/exit helpers; when the active game is networked, its ownerless test players use engine Sit/Eject directly. This fixture does not inject E or establish network RPC authorization, ownership or replication.

BikeRiders.cs checks all 12 bikes with authored grips: palm and saddle alignment, preserved arm lengths, clothing and bone attachments while moving and turning, and pose restoration after exit. It captures private engine renders of NRG500, Sanchez and BMX without changing the active scene or camera. Rider fitting has been tested with the Human avatar only.

CarOccupants.cs checks seven mounted car/truck/bus models in a disposable scene: driver palm contact, ankle targets, forward-facing feet, preserved limb lengths, unchanged player/chair/renderer transforms and normal animation after exit. Passenger arms remain in the ordinary seated pose. Unsupported floor targets are reported separately and checked for unchanged animation. It captures Infernus and Banshee renders; clothing checks run when an active avatar supplies clothing. This validates local Human poses, not input or multiplayer replication.

VehiclePresentation.cs runs the real native Tick with seated temporary players for a car, motorcycle, boat, plane and helicopter. It checks engine sound handles, decodes their actual engine-side samples to reject silence, and confirms rendered helicopter rotor bones rotate. This validates resource playback and presentation, not perceived volume or wall-clock loop persistence in the active map.

VehicleAudioLifecycle.cs checks an Infernus loop across 3.5 real seconds with a temporary listener first in the driver seat and then outside. It rejects handle restarts and checks the actual acoustic model does not mute the engine through the car's own hull. The listener and car live in a disposable scene; no active-map objects are changed.

VehicleAudioDrivetrain.cs plays the installed Infernus and Sanchez engine loops for 18 real seconds while a deterministic speed ramp holds the accelerator. It checks actual SoundHandle pitch falls at every source-gear upshift, continuous playback without restarts, and nonzero audio amplitude. This tests the audio transmission separately from player input and vehicle dynamics.

VehicleAudioFalloff.cs compares the live sound handle's gain at cabin and exterior distances. VehicleRotors.cs checks actual skin-weighted rotor geometry on nine full-size helicopters at rest and while spinning, and captures Maverick/Hunter engine renders. RoadBrakingSuspension.cs measures Infernus stopping distances, source spring equilibrium, tire contact and bounded wheel travel in an isolated physics scene.

VehicleAudioFalloff.cs checks the actual direct-sound attenuation curve with an isolated listener at 50, 300 and 1,000 units. It requires unchanged cabin gain and source volume, with less exterior attenuation for cars and helicopters.

VehicleRotors.cs checks all nine full-size helicopters using installed skin weights and actual rendered bone transforms. Rotor geometry must retain its span at rest and while spinning; empty legacy bone names cannot stand in for a geometry check. It also renders isolated Maverick and Hunter screenshots without moving the active camera.

VehicleSandboxAudio.cs exercises the official MountSpawner and automatic VehicleRuntime discovery with an owned temporary driver in the actual running Sandbox scene. It verifies the loop survives real frames and belongs to the existing listener's scene, then removes its temporary car and driver. Passing this check does not resolve a reported perceptual sound issue without reproducing it under the player's conditions.

VehicleControls.cs is a read-only ten-second observer for the real seated local player. Approach the driver's door and press E before starting it; E selects passenger seats from their nearby doors and also exits the current seat. Press the driving controls normally during its window; it records physical keys, controller input, motion, rotor state and audio playback without injecting input or moving the player. Any control with zero observed frames remains untested. VehiclePhysics.cs supplies synthetic controller state and does not establish physical-key success.

FirstSpawns.cs runs in the official Sandbox project during play. It uses Sandbox's real mount spawner for CJ, an Infernus and a bin, forces NPC/vehicle discovery before the first `OnStart`, and checks that the model stays visible without an install-game prompt. It removes its temporary objects afterward.

PedestrianNavigation.cs creates a temporary floor and mounted Big Smoke with the real native controller. It checks idle/retry behavior without a navmesh, resumes actual movement after a navmesh appears, abandons a cancelled path, rejects a route through a full-width wall, and moves again after the wall is removed. No active-scene or camera changes are made.

NativeAnimation.cs loads the mounted SKR_derrick01 native Unreal animation variant into a temporary scene and verifies four articulated joints change between sequence samples. This exercises native UE clip conversion and actual engine playback, independently of the legacy pedestrian IFP adapter.

PedestrianPhysics.cs checks CJ and Big Smoke's generated 16-body/15-joint posing rigs in an isolated scene: keyframed limb colliders follow animation, all bodies survive a six-second fall onto a floor, and moving/freezing one forearm leaves the other bodies connected. Configured mass is 80 kg; kinematic or frozen bodies correctly contribute zero to the engine's dynamic-mass total. Controller mode changes and pose serialization are tested separately.

PedestrianPosing.cs checks the real native controller's limb traces, first-grab transition, disabled navigation, persistent limb freezing, passive damage, root movement, and save/load of a frozen pose. It uses the same Rigidbody unfreeze operation as Sandbox's physgun, without synthesizing mouse input. It does not establish multiplayer behavior. PedestrianPhysics.cs separately checks the generated rig's body/joint geometry and stability.

PedestrianFirstFrame.cs spawns CJ and Big Smoke through built-in Props with a nonzero heading and scale. It verifies upright animation and kinematic limbs from the first rendered frame, plus recovery after a Prop has already simulated as a ragdoll. Navigation is disabled so it cannot mask an incorrect initial orientation.

PedestrianSpeech.cs plays original mounted Smoke and pain clips, including automatic ambient speech. It checks head positioning, cooldowns, recent-clip avoidance, crowd limits, damage/death reactions and cleanup during posing, disable and shutdown. It exercises local playback; speech replication to other clients is not implemented.

BulletTracers.cs traces a real wall, checks that streaks advance along the damage ray without crossing the hit point, compares engine renders, and verifies expiration, the crowd limit and scene cleanup. All objects live in a disposable scene.
